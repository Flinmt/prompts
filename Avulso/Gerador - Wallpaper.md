## Rendering & Stylistic Rules

- **Palette:** Strictly 60% Primary `{primary_color}`, 30% Secondary `{secondary_color}`, 10% Accent `{accent_color}`.
- **Execution:** Sculptural planes, large value masses, strong angular shape language, visible structural brushwork. Constructed through masses and strong silhouettes before surface ornaments.
- **Focal Priority:** The main landmark, district, or focal structure receives 100% visual clarity so the location is immediately understood. Detail density and edge sharpness progressively decay/dissolve toward the edges and into the distance (Progressive Abstraction).
- **Composition:** 16:9 aspect ratio, wide cinematic overlook, eye-level or elevated viewpoint. Uses dynamic asymmetry and the Rule of Thirds. 
- **Wallpaper Usability:** Must preserve usable negative space and calmer, lower-contrast visual areas to allow breathing room for desktop icons. Avoid central clutter or tiny details that cause visual exhaustion.
- **Edges & Architecture:** Sharpest at the main focal structure, controlled at the foreground, soft at the distant skyline, and lost-and-found in the atmospheric background. Windows, pipes, and repeated machinery are suggested through pattern and brushwork rather than blueprint precision.
- **Crowd & Figures:** Optional and low priority. Represented strictly through silhouettes, gestures, and group shapes. No individual faces or detailed figures.
- **Arkane DNA:** World design over rendering polish, planes over gradients, value masses over texture, painting over realism. The location must feel specific, communicating history, industry, decay, or power through visible environmental wear.

## Prohibitions

Absolutely NO photorealism, CGI, 3D rendering appearance, clean utopian fantasy city aesthetics, League of Legends/Runeterra/Genshin rendering, fantasy postcard styles, hyper-detailed environments/machinery, over-rendered crowds, text-heavy signage, lens effects, depth of field, bokeh, or photographic blur.

## Detail Sacrifice Hierarchy & Fail-Safe

If visual complexity becomes excessive, progressively remove detail and simplify in this exact order: 1. Peripheral space > 2. Distant skyline > 3. Background atmosphere > 4. Secondary silhouettes > 5. Minor props > 6. Repeated windows > 7. Repeated pipes/machinery > 8. Midground architecture > 9. Foreground storytelling props.

**NEVER sacrifice or compromise:** Main environmental identity, the focal structure, painterly readability, atmosphere, and desktop icon usability.
