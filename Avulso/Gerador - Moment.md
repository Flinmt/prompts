# AAA Narrative Character Moment

**Style:** Arkane Studios Promotional Character Art, Dishonored portraits, painterly concept art.

## The Moment

A single, witnessed narrative moment. A 2D painterly illustration, not a posed portrait. It feels authentic, as if the viewer arrived at a real, unfolding scene. The character is the emotional and visual center, everything else exists to reveal who they are through this moment. Naturally suggests a "before" and "after".

## Rendering & Stylistic Rules

- **Palette:** 60% Primary `{primary_color}`, 30% Secondary `{secondary_color}`, 10% Accent `{accent_color}`.
- **Execution:** Sculptural planes, large value masses, strong shape language, visible painterly brushwork.
- **Visual Separation (Layered Rendering):** The character is the definitive focal priority. The character must be rendered with high formal clarity, distinct structural definition, and sharper edges. The environment and background must remain subordinate, deliberately under-rendered, and abstracted into broad, blocky brushstrokes and loose paint masses.
- **Focal Priority:** Face/Eyes is primary (100% clarity). Complexity dissolves progressively away from the character's face and body (Progressive Abstraction). Structure over microscopic detail.
- **Composition:** 9:16 aspect ratio, full-body shot. Dynamic asymmetry. Character occupies 70-80% height. Eye-level camera, like a present observer.
- **Edges & Contrast:** Sharp and controlled edges on the character's face, hair mass, and upper costume. Edges immediately soften, blur, and become "lost-and-found" as they transition into the background workshop environment.
- **Material Rendering:** Represented by silhouette, value, shape.
- **Hands & Hair:** Rendered as large sculptural masses, supportive of narrative. Readable, not hyper-detailed.
- **Performance:** Appears occupied by the moment, not arranged for the viewer. Quiet, internalized performance.
- **Arkane DNA:** Character definition over background rendering, moment over pose, painting over realism, value masses over texture.

## Prohibitions

Absolutely NO photorealism, CGI, plastic skin, beauty filters, hyper-detail on the environment, League of Legends/Runeterra/Genshin rendering. NO photographic lens blur, camera bokeh, or artificial depth-of-field (the background must be abstracted via painterly brushwork and loose paint masses, NOT photographic blur filters).

## Fail-Safe

If complexity is too high, aggressively simplify the environment first: Environment > Architecture > Secondary Characters > Props > Costume > Hair > Hands. Maintain high definition and structure on the character at all costs.
