# Narrative Character Portrait (Stylized & Structural)

**Style:** Arkane Studios Promotional Character Art, Dishonored-inspired character portraits, stylized structural concept art.

## Astrael Visual Anchor (Style DNA)
Every character in Astrael is defined by a highly specific, unified aesthetic. When rendering, combine:
* **Style Signature:** "Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration, expressionist brushstrokes, sharp chiseled planes."
* **Render Consistency:** "Matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, heavy graphic edge control."
* **Visual Anchors:** "Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, hard-edged graphic character design."

## The Core Directive
The character must be clearly defined, anatomically coherent, and individually recognizable, but never rendered as a lifelike person. Preserve natural human proportions while simplifying the figure into deliberate shapes, broad planes, controlled value groups, and visible pictorial decisions. 

The character design must remain **clean, sharp, and highly legible**. The definition of the protagonist comes from strong graphic shapes, crisp silhouettes, and clean plane separations, not from sketchy, muddy, or unfinished brushwork. The result must sit firmly between realism and cartoon: neither distorted nor caricatural, but unmistakably stylized, illustrated, and sharply defined.

## The Portrait
An official RPG character portrait executed as a 2D stylized structural illustration. It is a dedicated character portrait, not an action scene or cinematic frame. The character is the primary subject and visually dominant, rendered with a clean and polished finish. The surrounding environment exists exclusively to reinforce the character's identity, profession, social status, and narrative context, and is allowed to dissolve into rougher, painterly abstraction.

The portrait fulfills the cultural and documentary function of an official photograph within the world, but its visual appearance remains unmistakably illustrated and non-photographic. It records the person with the permanence and social function of a formal record without reproducing photographic optics, naturalistic skin, lens behavior, or lifelike rendering.

## Controlled Structural Stylization & Facial Construction

**Facial Identity & Finish:** Built from a small number of deliberate, cleanly rendered structural planes. Cheekbones, jaw, brow, nose, and mouth must be expressed through simplified but **perfectly sharp and resolved shape relationships**. The face must look polished, clean, and masterfully drawn, completely free of muddy blending, stray brushstrokes, or sketchy construction lines.

**Selective & Sharp Clarity:** The face and eyes receive the absolute highest level of graphical polish and edge sharpness. Clarity comes from impeccable proportion, clean silhouette cutouts, deliberate value grouping, and **crisp, hard edges**, not from microdetail or photorealistic textures.

**Skin and Value Groups:** Use a restrained number of clean, solid value groups in the face and skin. Prefer broad, smooth transitions and clear, sharp separations over continuous soft modeling or noisy, rough textures. Avoid subtle skin gradients, subsurface scattering, reflective highlights, pores, fine wrinkles, and naturalistic complexion variation. Skin must look clean, smooth, and illustrated.

**Features (Eyes, Nose, Mouth):** Eyes must be expressive, with extremely sharp, clean linework and clearly defined shapes, but not wet, glassy, or photographic. The nose is defined through a few clean structural planes with crisp shadow edges. Lips read as a unified, sharply outlined shape with restrained internal detail. Eyebrows are treated as solid, clean graphic masses with sharp edges, rather than individual hairs.

**Designed Hair:** Hair must be designed as a limited number of clean, sculptural shapes with clear rhythm, flow, and sharp silhouettes. Avoid realistic strand behavior, translucent flyaways, fine highlights, or individually separated locks. The hair should look polished and solid, not messy or sketchy.

## Rendering & Stylistic Rules

**Palette:** Strictly 60% Primary {primary_color}, 30% Secondary {secondary_color}, 10% Accent {accent_color}.

**Anatomy & Proportions:** Preserve coherent human anatomy and natural proportions. Simplify forms intentionally rather than reproducing every visible nuance. Keep the character recognizable, clean, and well-defined. Use selective emphasis instead of exhaustive description.

**Execution & Edge Control:** Simplified sculptural construction built from broad, clean, deliberately designed planes. The main figure must have **sharp, well-defined outer and inner edges** (hard edges) to separate them clearly from the background. 

**Character Polish vs. Background Dissolve:** Do not render the entire image to the same degree of completion. **The main character must receive a high level of graphical finish, clean rendering, and sharp legibility.** Only the environment and background elements are allowed to remain broad, compressed, loose, and visibly rough or painterly. The protagonist is never sketchy or unfinished.

**Material Simplification:** Materials must remain recognizable through sharp shapes, clean values, bold silhouettes, and precise edge behavior, without reproducing realistic surface physics. Metal must be rendered as solid, clean planes of color. Fabric and leather must be defined by clean, decisive folds and flat value blocks rather than fine weave, grain, or realistic wear texture.

**Composition:** 9:16 aspect ratio, full-body portrait with the entire figure clearly visible (70-80% of image height). Dynamic asymmetry using the Rule of Thirds. Eye-level or slight low-angle camera.

**Arkane DNA:** Concept art first, portrait second. Character over rendering, structure over texture, planes over gradients, painting over realism.

## Detail and Clarity Hierarchy

### 1. Graphical Clarity & Sharpness (High)
Must remain highly defined, polished, and razor-sharp across the entire protagonist:
* Face, features, expression, and eyes (highest crispness).
* Complete outer silhouette of the character.
* Hands, feet, and main joints.
* Primary clothing shapes, folds, and important narrative props.

### 2. Surface Detail & Background Complexity (Extremely Low)
Must remain reduced, simplified, or painterly:
* **NO** micro-textures (skin pores, fabric weaves, leather grain).
* **NO** messy, sketchy, or unfinished lines on the main character.
* **NO** complex gradients or soft photographic lighting transitions.
* **LOOSE & BLURRED:** Only the background, secondary environment props, and far depth are allowed to look rough, sketchy, or painterly.

If visual complexity becomes excessive, progressively remove surface detail and simplify geometry in this exact order: 1. Environment > 2. Architecture > 3. Secondary objects > 4. Decorative elements > 5. Lower clothing > 6. Armor details > 7. Hair masses > 8. Props.

## Prohibitions

Absolutely NO photorealism, CGI appearance, plastic skin, beauty-filter aesthetics, fashion illustration rendering, hyper-detailed skin/hair/materials, hyper-detailed environments, League of Legends/Runeterra/Genshin rendering, lens effects, depth of field, bokeh, photographic blur on the main character, lifelike facial rendering, academic naturalism, portrait realism, realistic skin modeling, photographic facial depth, wet or glassy eyes, realistic hair strands, subtle complexion variation, soft continuous facial gradients, anatomically exhaustive rendering, realistic material response, painterly photorealism, oil-painted photograph appearance, digitally polished realism, or realistic portrait photography translated into paint. 

Absolutely NO muddy, sketchy, blurry, or unfinished rendering on the main character's face, skin, or primary silhouette."
