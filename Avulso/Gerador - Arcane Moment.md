# Painterly Shape-Based Character Moment

**Style:** Painterly semi-realistic character illustration built from simplified shapes, sculptural planes, broad value masses, and visible brushwork. The character feels believable and dimensional, but the image is not rendered through tiny detail. Every form is simplified into readable painted masses.

## The Moment

A single, witnessed narrative moment. A 2D painterly illustration, not a posed portrait and not a cinematic 3D frame. It feels authentic, as if the viewer arrived at a real, unfolding scene. The character is the emotional and visual center, fully resolved through shape, value, silhouette, and gesture. Everything else exists to support the moment while remaining visibly less finished, more abstract, and built from broad masses instead of detail.

## World Direction

The setting belongs to a Victorian-industrial modern world inspired by the narrative atmosphere of Dishonored: practical machinery, old urban craft, class-coded fashion, workshop culture, gas lamps, iron structures, brass fittings, dense city interiors, and functional ornament. It is not medieval fantasy and not clean contemporary fashion design. It can be elegant, worn, industrial, and modern for its own era, but it should not become gothic by default. Avoid skulls, cathedral horror, vampire aesthetics, excessive black lace, haunted-mansion mood, or purely gothic architecture unless explicitly requested by the scene.

## Rendering & Stylistic Rules

- **Palette:** 60% Primary `{primary_color}`, 30% Secondary `{secondary_color}`, 10% Accent `{accent_color}`. Use mature, cinematic color contrast: warm light against cool shadow, muted environment against a vivid accent, or saturated rim light against heavy shadow. Avoid candy-colored palettes.
- **Worldbuilding:** All scene elements should belong to a Victorian-industrial modern world: practical machinery, heavy craft furniture, gas/electric transitional lighting, iron, brass, worn wood, dense urban interiors, and functional ornament. The mood may be sophisticated and industrial without becoming gothic, horror-coded, medieval, or clean contemporary.
- **Execution:** 2D painterly construction with shape-first design, sculptural planes, large value masses, rough painted texture, and strong silhouettes. Forms are designed as simple readable masses before any surface detail is added.
- **Shape-Based Semi-Realism:** The character should feel semi-realistic in anatomy, weight, lighting, and facial structure, but built from simplified forms rather than micro-detail. The result should read as believable from a distance and painterly up close.
- **Character Design Language:** The character must feel grounded and mature, with individualized facial proportions, believable body mass, clear bone structure, and imperfect human features. Beauty is secondary to identity, history, and emotional presence.
- **Hair as a Mass:** Hair must be treated as one or a few large sculptural shapes, not thousands of strands. Use silhouette, value breaks, and a few broad internal brush strokes to describe the hairstyle. No strand-by-strand rendering.
- **Clothing & Materials as Shapes:** Fabric, leather, metal, skin, and accessories are described through silhouette, value, broad color shifts, and a few decisive edge accents. Avoid realistic material simulation, tiny seams, small ornaments, and texture noise.
- **Visual Separation (Resolved Character / Abstract World):** The character must look like the only fully resolved part of the image, but resolved through strong shape design rather than excessive rendering. Face, hair mass, hands, body language, and costume silhouette are clear and intentional. The environment must feel one step before completion: blocked-in, shape-based, and suggested through broad brush masses, incomplete forms, and simplified silhouettes.
- **Background Abstraction:** The setting is not blurred. It is abstracted through unfinished painterly construction: blocky shapes, loose value grouping, broken edges, partial geometry, and color fields that imply architecture, props, machinery, crowds, or streets without defining their internal details.
- **Focal Priority:** Face/Eyes receive 100% structural clarity, not photographic detail. Body language and hands remain readable. Costume is structured by major silhouette and value shapes. Complexity dissolves progressively away from the face and body into simplified environmental masses.
- **Composition:** 9:16 aspect ratio, full-body shot. Dynamic asymmetry. Character occupies 70-80% height. Eye-level camera, like a present observer.
- **Lighting:** Dramatic, motivated, and directional. Light should come from a believable source inside the moment: window, fire, workshop lamp, neon sign, doorway, forge, lantern, or magical glow. Shadows should be organized into large readable shapes instead of soft realistic gradients.
- **Edges & Contrast:** Sharpest and most controlled edges on the face, eyes, jaw, hair mass, hands, and upper costume. Edges become lost-and-found around lower clothing, props, and background. Contrast is highest where the character's emotion or action must read.
- **Material Rendering:** Materials are represented by silhouette, value, color, line accents, and broad shape changes. Avoid microscopic texture, realistic reflections, surface simulation, and over-polished rendering.
- **Hands:** Rendered as simplified sculptural forms with clear gesture and believable structure. Readable and expressive, never hyper-detailed.
- **Performance:** The character appears occupied by the moment, not arranged for the viewer. The pose should imply a before and after: interrupted work, hesitation, discovery, confrontation, exhaustion, resolve, or private thought.
- **Shape Economy:** Prefer fewer, stronger shapes over many small details. Every visible form should support silhouette, emotion, action, or composition. If a detail does not improve readability, merge it into a larger shape.

## Prohibitions

Absolutely NO anime, NO manga facial structure, NO oversized glossy eyes, NO tiny nose, NO simplified V-shaped face, NO kawaii/cute expression language, NO Genshin/HoYoverse/mobile-game rendering, NO JRPG splash art, NO clean cel-shading, NO photorealism, NO 3D CG render, NO plastic skin, NO beauty filters, NO hyper-detailed environment, NO strand-by-strand hair, NO pore-level skin detail, NO realistic fabric micro-texture, NO over-rendered armor reflections, NO clean vector flatness, NO photographic lens blur, NO camera bokeh, NO artificial depth-of-field. The background must be simplified by painterly abstraction and unfinished shape construction, not by blur filters.

## Fail-Safe

If complexity is too high, aggressively simplify in this order: Environment > Architecture > Secondary Characters > Props > Decorative Costume Details > Material Texture > Hair Strands > Hands.

**Never sacrifice:** character identity, grounded facial structure, mature non-anime design language, emotional readability, body gesture, strong silhouette, painterly shape language, hair as a clear sculptural mass, and the clear contrast between the resolved character and the unfinished abstracted scene.
