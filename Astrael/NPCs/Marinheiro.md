# Narrative Character Portrait (Stylized & Structural)

A vertical 2D character portrait in a Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration style. The rendering features a matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, and heavy graphic edge control. Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, and hard-edged graphic character design with expressionist brushstrokes and sharp chiseled planes. Absolutely no photorealism, CGI, 3D render, smooth plastic skin, or digital blending.

## Astrael Visual Anchor (Style DNA)
Every character in Astrael is defined by a highly specific, unified aesthetic. When rendering, combine:
Style Signature: "Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration, crisp high-fidelity focal points, sharp chiseled planes with high-contrast directional lighting."
Render Consistency: "Matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, clean graphic edge control, with clear luminous highlights and sharp shadow planes to prevent muddy or dark-filtered overlays."
Visual Anchors: "Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, hard-edged graphic character design, stylized and recognizable but simplified environments."

## Subject & Pose:
A 50-year-old weathered sailor at the Véspera industrial port. He stands in a full-body 9:16 vertical crop, dominating the frame. He leans sideways against a heavy iron mooring bollard, one arm crossed over his chest, the other hand gripping a coiled rope at his side. He looks directly at the viewer with a flat, tired forward gaze.

## Facial Construction (Astrael DNA):
His face is constructed strictly from hand-painted structural planes, a broad chiseled jaw, prominent brow bone, and rough cheekbone planes, completely free of smooth gradients or soft skin textures. He has a tight, unsmiling mouth pulled slightly to one side. His eyes are faded grey-blue with zero glassy reflections or moisture. His hair is a solid, short dark mass with carved, sharp edges and no individual strands, receding slightly at the temples.

## Clothing & Materials:
He wears a heavy thick wool peacoat in tar black with wide lapels and large brass buttons, left open over a worn faded ochre canvas shirt with the collar unbuttoned. Faded grey canvas trousers tucked into heavy scuffed leather boots with a thick leather belt and a simple rounded brass buckle. All clothing folds are rendered as flat, geometric shapes of color with hard, clean edges.

## Background & Palette:
The background is an industrial port dock with a ship hull, wooden pilings, and stacked cargo cratesrendered in a simplified blocky graphic style with clean, flat paint shapes and bold silhouettes, maintaining recognizable structures while omitting fine details and micro-textures. Color palette is strictly 60% Tar Black and Iron Grey (coat, boots, shadows), 30% Faded Ochre and Weathered Canvas (shirt, skin, dock wood), and 10% Rusted Bronze and Faded Blue (buttons, eyes).
