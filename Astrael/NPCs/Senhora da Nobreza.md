# Narrative Character Portrait (Stylized & Structural)

A vertical 2D character portrait in a Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration style. The rendering features a matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, and heavy graphic edge control. Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, and hard-edged graphic character design with expressionist brushstrokes and sharp chiseled planes. Absolutely no photorealism, CGI, 3D render, smooth plastic skin, or digital blending.

## Astrael Visual Anchor (Style DNA)
Every character in Astrael is defined by a highly specific, unified aesthetic. When rendering, combine:
Style Signature: "Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration, crisp high-fidelity focal points, sharp chiseled planes with high-contrast directional lighting."
Render Consistency: "Matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, clean graphic edge control, with clear luminous highlights and sharp shadow planes to prevent muddy or dark-filtered overlays."
Visual Anchors: "Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, hard-edged graphic character design, stylized and recognizable but simplified environments."

## Subject & Pose:
A 70-year-old noblewoman in her private library in Véspera. She stands in a full-body 9:16 vertical crop, dominating the frame. She poses next to a tall wooden bookshelf, one hand resting on the spine of a book, the other holding a pair of reading glasses at waist level. She looks directly at the viewer with a flat, appraising forward gaze, chin lifted slightly.

## Facial Construction (Astrael DNA):
Her face is constructed strictly from hand-painted structural planes, sharp aged cheekbones, a defined jaw with slight jowl planes, and a straight narrow nose bridge, completely free of smooth gradients or soft skin textures. She has a tight, unsmiling mouth with thin lips. Her eyes are sharp pale grey with zero glassy reflections or moisture. Her hair is a solid, sculptural mass of silver-grey pinned into an elaborate bun with carved, sharp outer edges and no individual strands.

## Clothing & Materials:
She wears a highly structured floor-length gown in deep burgundy velvet with a high lace collar in cream, long fitted sleeves with crisp cuffs, and flat geometric folds in the skirt. A simple black jet brooch is pinned at her collar. Dark fitted gloves are partially pulled off her right hand, exposing her fingers. Low heeled velvet shoes.

## Background & Palette:
The background is a private library with tall bookshelves, a writing desk, and a warm gas lamp, rendered in a simplified blocky graphic style with clean, flat paint shapes and bold silhouettes, maintaining recognizable structures while omitting fine details and micro-textures. Color palette is strictly 60% Deep Burgundy and Forest Green (gown, shadows, books), 30% Faded Gold and Ivory (pages, lace collar, skin, lamp glow), and 10% Jet Black and Silver (brooch, glasses frame, eyes).
