# Narrative Character Portrait (Stylized & Structural)

A vertical 2D character portrait in a Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration style. The rendering features a matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, and heavy graphic edge control. Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, and hard-edged graphic character design with expressionist brushstrokes and sharp chiseled planes. Absolutely no photorealism, CGI, 3D render, smooth plastic skin, or digital blending.

## Astrael Visual Anchor (Style DNA)
Every character in Astrael is defined by a highly specific, unified aesthetic. When rendering, combine:
Style Signature: "Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration, crisp high-fidelity focal points, sharp chiseled planes."
Render Consistency: "Matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, clean graphic edge control on the character, simplified blocky structures with visible brushwork on the background."
Visual Anchors: "Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, hard-edged graphic character design, stylized and recognizable but simplified environments."

## Subject & Pose:
A 24-year-old red-haired woman, a haute couture model from a Véspera fashion house. She stands in a full-body 9:16 vertical crop, dominating the frame. She poses next to a wooden tailoring mannequin, one hand resting on its shoulder, the other hand placed on her own hip. She looks directly at the viewer with a flat, unsmiling forward gaze, chin slightly lifted.

## Facial Construction (Astrael DNA):
Her face is constructed strictly from hand-painted structural planes, sharp chiseled jawlines, and prominent cheekbone blocks, completely free of smooth gradients or soft skin textures. She has a neutral, closed mouth. Her eyes are sharp pale grey with zero glassy reflections or moisture. Her hair is a solid, carved mass of deep crimson red, voluminous but shaped as a single sculptural block with clean outer edges and no individual strands.

## Clothing & Materials:
She wears a highly structured deep burgundy tailored jacket with stiff lapels over a crisp warm-ivory blouse with a high collar, sleeves fitted at the wrist. A fitted dark charcoal skirt falls in clean geometric folds. Tall polished black leather boots with simplified metal closures. A thin leather strap wraps once around her wrist as the only accessory.

## Background & Palette:
The background is a compact private atelier with a cutting table and paper patterns, rendered in a simplified blocky graphic style with clean, flat paint shapes and bold silhouettes, maintaining recognizable structures while omitting fine details and micro-textures. Color palette is strictly 60% Deep Burgundy and Charcoal (jacket, skirt, shadows), 30% Warm Ivory and Pale Stone (blouse, mannequin, skin), and 10% Oxidized Brass and Grey (closures, eyes).
