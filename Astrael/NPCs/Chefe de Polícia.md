# Narrative Character Portrait (Stylized & Structural)

A vertical 2D character portrait in a Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration style. The rendering features a matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, and heavy graphic edge control. Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, and hard-edged graphic character design with expressionist brushstrokes and sharp chiseled planes. Absolutely no photorealism, CGI, 3D render, smooth plastic skin, or digital blending.

## Astrael Visual Anchor (Style DNA)
Every character in Astrael is defined by a highly specific, unified aesthetic. When rendering, combine:
Style Signature: "Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration, crisp high-fidelity focal points, sharp chiseled planes with high-contrast directional lighting."
Render Consistency: "Matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, clean graphic edge control, with clear luminous highlights and sharp shadow planes to prevent muddy or dark-filtered overlays."
Visual Anchors: "Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, hard-edged graphic character design, stylized and recognizable but simplified environments."

## Subject & Pose:
A 55-year-old police department chief from Véspera. He stands in a full-body 9:16 vertical crop, dominating the frame. He stands behind a heavy wooden desk, both hands placed flat on its surface, shoulders squared. He leans slightly forward, looking directly at the viewer with a flat, stern, unwavering forward gaze.

## Facial Construction (Astrael DNA):
His face is constructed strictly from hand-painted structural planes, a broad heavy jaw, prominent brow ridge, and wide cheekbone planes, completely free of smooth gradients or soft skin textures. He has a tight, unsmiling straight mouth. His eyes are sharp dark brown with zero glassy reflections or moisture. His hair is a solid, short neatly combed mass of dark grey with carved, sharp edges and no individual strands.

## Clothing & Materials:
He wears a highly structured midnight blue police coat with a high stiff collar, polished brass buttons running in a double row, and a matching dark grey waistcoat beneath with a simple silver chain across the front. A crisp white shirt with a stiff collar. Dark fitted trousers and polished black leather boots with simplified metal closures. A simple silver badge is pinned to his chest.

## Background & Palette:
The background is a police office with a wooden desk, stacked case files, and a gas lamp, rendered in a simplified blocky graphic style with clean, flat paint shapes and bold silhouettes, maintaining recognizable structures while omitting fine details and micro-textures.. Color palette is strictly 60% Midnight Blue and Black (coat, desk, shadows), 30% Warm Grey and Ivory (waistcoat, shirt, papers, skin), and 10% Polished Brass and Silver (buttons, badge, chain).
