# Narrative Character Portrait (Stylized & Structural)

A vertical 2D character portrait in a Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration style. The rendering features a matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, and heavy graphic edge control. Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, and hard-edged graphic character design with expressionist brushstrokes and sharp chiseled planes. Absolutely no photorealism, CGI, 3D render, smooth plastic skin, or digital blending.

## Astrael Visual Anchor (Style DNA)
Every character in Astrael is defined by a highly specific, unified aesthetic. When rendering, combine:
Style Signature: "Leyendecker-inspired angular structural illustration, crisp high-fidelity focal points, sharp chiseled planes with high-contrast directional lighting."
Render Consistency: "Matte gouache texture, solid paint blocks, clean graphic edge control, with clear luminous highlights and sharp shadow planes to prevent muddy or dark-filtered overlays."
Visual Anchors: "Strictly no gradients, zero photographic blending, hard-edged graphic character design, stylized and recognizable but simplified environments." 

## Subject & Pose:
A 40-year-old Void Observer of Indian ancestry, with warm brown skin. He stands in a full-body 9:16 vertical crop, dominating the frame. He poses on a heavy, solid broken stone coliseum block, looking directly at the viewer with an intense, unwavering forward gaze. His hands are concealed behind his back, forming a clean, bold, and mysterious silhouette.

## Facial Construction (Astrael DNA):
His face is constructed strictly from hand-painted structural planes, sharp chiseled jawlines, and prominent cheekbone blocks, completely free of smooth gradients or soft skin textures. He has a neutral, unsmiling mouth. His eyes are solid, entirely black (merged sclera and iris) with zero glassy reflections or moisture. His hair is a solid, swept-back dark mass with carved, sharp edges and no individual strands.

## Clothing & Materials:
He wears a highly structured, high-collar black bandhgala vest over a dark shirt with sleeves rolled and folded to the mid-forearm. Fitted charcoal trousers and heavy, worn leather boots. All clothing folds are rendered as flat, geometric shapes of color with hard, clean edges.

## Background & Palette:
The background is a deep, weightless night sky with a few highly abstracted, floating debris shapes, rendered in a simplified blocky graphic style with clean, flat paint shapes and bold silhouettes, maintaining recognizable structures while omitting fine details and micro-textures. Color palette is strictly 60% Midnight Black and Indigo (vest, shadows), 30% Warm Charcoal and Bronze (boots, skin), and 10% Ash Grey and Brass (highlights on the stone).
